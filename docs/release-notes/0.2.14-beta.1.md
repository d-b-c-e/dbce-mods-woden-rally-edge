Super Woden: Rally Edge with a racing wheel: wheel and pedal controls, force feedback for
any DirectInput wheel, Forza-format telemetry for SimHub, bonnet and bumper cameras, and
angle-correct triple screens on NVIDIA Surround or three separate monitors.

**This is an early-tester build.** It has been driven on one rig (MOZA R12, three
2560x1440 screens). Please open an issue with your wheel, screen layout and what happened,
good or bad.

## Install

1. Close the game. Download **WodenRallyEdge-0.2.14-beta.1.zip** below and extract the
   whole ZIP into a new folder.
2. Double-click **Install.bat**. It finds the Steam game, backs up what it replaces, keeps
   your settings and, on first install, downloads the supported BepInEx loader and checks
   its hash.
3. Launch from Steam. **F6** opens the settings panel: bind and calibrate the wheel and pedals
   on Controls, pick the wheel on FFB. **F8** turns force feedback off.

The README in the ZIP has the details, including triple-screen setup. **Uninstall.bat**
removes only the mod's own files.

## What's in it

- Steering, throttle, brake and an analog handbrake from any USB devices; sequential shift
  up/down on any button; menus from wheel buttons.
- Force feedback from the tyre forces.
- Telemetry in the Forza Horizon "Data Out" format, on by default.
- Bonnet and Bumper cameras in the game's camera cycle; numpad moves the camera.
- Triple screens with the HUD and menus on the centre screen. On three separate monitors the
  mod opens one borderless window across them by itself.
- The optional countdown-timer assist keeps those runs off the Steam leaderboards.

## Known limits

- Force feedback strength is not yet matched to our other mods' 50% scale.
- The bumper camera's field of view looks a little off.
- No H-pattern shifter or clutch yet.
- Only the current Steam build of the game (build 21802346) is supported; the installer
  refuses other builds.
