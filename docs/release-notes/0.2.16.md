Super Woden: Rally Edge with a racing wheel: wheel and pedal controls, force feedback for
any DirectInput wheel, Forza-format telemetry for SimHub, bonnet and bumper cameras, and
angle-correct triple screens on NVIDIA Surround or three separate monitors.

Developed on one rig (MOZA R12, three 2560x1440 screens). Please open an issue with your wheel,
screen layout and what happened, good or bad.

## Install

1. Close the game. Download **WodenRallyEdge-0.2.16.zip** below and extract the whole ZIP
   into a new folder.
2. Double-click **Install.bat**. It finds the Steam game, backs up what it replaces, keeps
   your settings and, on first install, downloads the supported BepInEx loader and checks
   its hash. Updating from beta.1 or beta.2 works the same way.
3. Launch from Steam. **F6** opens the settings panel: bind and calibrate the wheel and pedals
   on Controls, pick the wheel on FFB. **F8** turns force feedback off.

**Uninstall.bat** removes only the mod's own files.

## New since beta.2

- **Force feedback** builds and lightens with the front tyres' grip, with art of rally's
  return to centre, on the same 50% scale as our other mods. A crash kick on wall and car hits.
- **H-pattern shifter** on any USB device (needs the game's manual transmission), with neutral
  and reverse; a gearbox-at-start choice.
- **Fixed: the car sometimes would not drive** because a pedal resting just above its calibrated
  rest held a little brake. Pedals now always have a small rest deadzone, and F6 has a
  deadzone editor.
- Wheel button names in the game's A/B/Start prompts; wheel works in photo mode.
- One triple-screen selector (Off / Surround / Separate monitors); the game window always lands
  on the triple screens; matched picture across the seams.
- Telemetry adds lap, position and lap/race times; speed and gear match the HUD.
- F6 reworked: page tabs, clearer setup guidance, reliable closing, frame-rate readout, and a
  Help page that writes a redacted support report.

## Known limits

- No clutch.
- The wheel cannot press the game's X/Y-only menu actions (extra info, delete save slot), so
  those prompts keep the pad glyph.
- Only the current Steam build of the game (build 21802346) is supported; the installer
  refuses other builds.
