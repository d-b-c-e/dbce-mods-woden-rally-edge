  the HUD showing, press F11 twice and say so on ISS-0037.
- **Wheel feel:** force feedback in a stage. It was off for this run.
- **Wheel buttons:** dismiss the message of the day and the title with the wheel's Confirm and Start buttons, then move
  through the map screen and the Arcade menus with the D-pad.
- **Menu side screens:** the map and Arcade menus fill the side screens with the game's own background art (large
  yellow, green and cyan "Arcade" lettering). Say whether that looks right to you.

  `Live` a few seconds later. The line is logged when the shim opens. Wheel input worked in this run, but the line
